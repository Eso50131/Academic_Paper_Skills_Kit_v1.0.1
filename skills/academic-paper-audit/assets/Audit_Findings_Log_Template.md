## Status definitions

- **Open:** not yet addressed.
- **Accepted:** user accepts the finding and plans to address it.
- **Resolved:** the correction/evidence has been checked and the issue is resolved.
- **Disputed:** user disputes the finding; retain the rationale and evidence.
- **Unverifiable:** still cannot be checked because necessary evidence is unavailable.

Do not mark a finding resolved because a sentence was reworded if the underlying evidence problem remains.
